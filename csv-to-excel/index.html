    How it works

    The tool reads your CSV in the browser, detects the separator and the text encoding, shows you the first rows exactly as they will appear, and writes an .xlsx file. Nothing is guessed about dates, and numbers are only converted if you ask.

    Questions

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. Your files are read and processed by your own browser. Nothing is sent to Rinveta or to any server, and no file content or file name is ever recorded. You can switch off your internet connection after the page has loaded and the tool keeps working. The only thing we count is how often each button is used, in aggregate (no cookies, no profiles).

    Are the separator and encoding detected automatically?

    Yes. Comma, semicolon, tab and pipe separators are detected, and the file is read as UTF-8 or, if that fails, Windows-1252, so accented letters such as è, ü and ñ come out right. The summary tells you what was detected.

    Will it change my dates and numbers?

    By default nothing is converted: every value is written to Excel exactly as it appears in the CSV, as text. Codes with leading zeros (007), phone numbers, long IDs and dates such as 03/11/2026 therefore stay as they are. Excel may show a small green triangle on numbers stored as text; that is expected.

    Can I get real Excel numbers for calculations?

    Tick “Turn plain numbers into Excel numbers”. Only values that are unmistakably plain numbers are converted: an optional minus, digits without leading zeros, an optional decimal point, up to 15 digits, and reading back identically (so 1.50 stays text rather than becoming 1.5). Dates, codes with leading zeros, values with a decimal comma (1,5) and everything else stay text.

    Why are dates not converted?

    A date like 03/11/2026 means 3 November in some countries and 11 March in others. Guessing wrong would silently corrupt your data, so dates are always left exactly as written.

    What goes into the Excel file?

    One sheet, named after your file, with the same rows and columns as the CSV. If your first row is a header, nothing special is applied to it; it stays the first row.

    How big can the file be?

    Files of tens of thousands of rows work fine on a normal computer. Very large files depend on your browser memory.
