You have a statement as a PDF and need it in Excel: one row per transaction, amounts as numbers, totals that match the original. We extract the table, check it, and hand back one clean workbook.

Questions

Is my bank statement safe to send?

For the free preview, send one page with names and account numbers blanked out. Only if you want the full delivery do we confirm the order in writing, with seller details and terms, before any payment. We never need access to your bank account or online banking.

Does it work with my bank's layout?

Most text-based statements can be read, but the reader has to be adapted to each layout. That is exactly what the free preview shows: the first rows of your result, before you pay anything.

What about scanned statements?

Scanned or photographed pages need OCR, which is not included in the fixed prices. Tell us in the preview request and we quote it separately.
