Privacy notice

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who is responsible

The data controller is the individual who runs Rinveta. Contact for any privacy matter, including a request for the controller's full identity details: privacy@rinveta.com.

This website

This website sets no cookies and does no profiling. We count visits in aggregate with GoatCounter (no cookies, no personal profiles): your browser loads a script from gc.zgo.at and reports the page view to goatcounter.com. There are no other third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.); like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when you load a page, under its own privacy statement.

When you write to us

If you email hello@rinveta.com (or another @rinveta.com address) we process your email address, the content of your message and any attachments, only to reply, prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, deliver the work. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) and, for ordinary correspondence, our legitimate interest in answering (art. 6(1)(f)).

Your files

Please send files only after we have agreed on the job. Files are used only for your job, are not used to train AI models and are deleted 30 days after you accept the delivery, unless the law requires us to keep something. Do not send files containing special categories of data (health, etc.) or payment card details. We do not process bank statements, payslips, health data or ID documents.

Retention

Unanswered or declined inquiries are deleted within 12 months. Data tied to an order is kept for the period required by accounting and tax law.

Sharing

We do not sell data. Email is handled by our mail provider (OVH, France/EU). We share data with others only where needed to perform the job or required by law.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its processing or to receive it in a portable format, and you can object to processing, by writing to privacy@rinveta.com. You may complain to your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it).
