Questions

Which PDFs work best?

Price lists, product catalogues, reports and supplier lists where you can select the text and the table has a regular layout. Most can be read, but the reader has to be adapted to each layout. That is exactly what the free preview shows: the first rows of your result, before you pay anything.

Is it safe to send my file?

For the free preview, send one page. Only if you want the full delivery do we confirm the order in writing, with seller details and terms, before any payment. We never need access to your accounts or your computer.

What about scanned PDFs?

Scanned or photographed pages need OCR, which is not included in the fixed prices. Tell us in the preview request and we quote it separately.

What kinds of files can't you process?

We do not process bank statements, payslips, health data or ID documents. For the free preview, blank out any personal details before sending.
