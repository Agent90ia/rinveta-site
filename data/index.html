Rinveta Data

Data cleanup and Excel / CSV / PDF automation

For small offices that lose hours copying, merging and fixing files. Send a messy file, or describe a task you repeat every week or month. You get back a clean result — or a small, tested Python script that does the job for you whenever you need it.

What we do

  • Clean up CSV and Excel files: dates, duplicates, email and phone formats, blank rows
  • Combine many files with the same layout into one workbook
  • Weekly or monthly summary reports with totals, pivot tables and charts
  • Pull tables and fields out of text-based PDFs into Excel

What you receive: the finished file; the commented script, if you want something reusable; a short guide to running it; and a before/after check with row counts, so you can see what changed.

Fixed prices, no VAT added

  • Single file — €19 One PDF, Excel or CSV up to 10 pages or 2,000 rows, delivered as one clean Excel file. Within one business day.
  • Batch — €49 Up to 50 pages or 5 files with the same layout, merged into one workbook, with a before/after check.
  • Monthly — €79 / month For offices that receive the same kind of PDF every month (price lists, reports, invoices): up to 300 pages, same template, delivered within one business day. Month by month, no automatic renewal.

Free preview first: send one anonymised page or a small sample and you get back the first rows of the result at no cost and no obligation.
