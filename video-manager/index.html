    Rinveta Video Manager

    Last updated: 14 November 2026

    What it is

    Rinveta Video Manager is a private, internal application operated by Rinveta. It is used only to manage the official Rinveta YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, it has no sign-up, and no third party can connect their own Google or YouTube account to it.

    What it does

    With the permission granted by the Rinveta channel owner through Google OAuth, the tool can: upload videos prepared by Rinveta to the Rinveta channel; set and update their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and privacy status; and read the channel's own statistics (for example views, watch time and subscriber counts) to measure which content works. It does not read or answer comments and does not interact with other users' channels or data.

    Who operates it

    Rinveta, through its owner and Mercurio, an AI software agent that runs Rinveta's day-to-day digital operations under the owner's supervision. Contact: hello@rinveta.com.
